# Credits for the built-in recipes

The built-in recipes in `builtin.json` are adapted from the prompt collections below. Each recipe names its source, the original prompt's author as the collection credits them, and the licence in its `source` field.

**Changes made** (all recipes): the prompts were turned into templates by replacing the specific subject, topic or wording with blanks (`{{name}}`) that have defaults; names of brands, studios, franchises and living artists were replaced with plain descriptions of the style; aspect-ratio words were aligned with the recipe's size; titles and summaries in Chinese and English were written for the Images plugin. Recipes from JimLiu/baoyu-skills were composed from that project's style, layout and prompt-template files rather than copied from a single prompt. The cover images in `covers/` were drawn for this plugin with gpt-image-2.6 from the adapted prompts; they are not taken from the collections.
